# Example venue policy for satellite LLM systems

Rolling primary window: three years. Last 12 months: provisional candidates may include lower-tier recent work, workshops, early access, and preprints. Keep direct novelty threats regardless of venue, clearly marked.

Example A venues to verify against current official proceedings: INFOCOM, MobiCom, MobiSys, SIGCOMM, NSDI, OSDI, SOSP, MLSys, and directly relevant NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR main conferences. The list is field-specific and is not an automatic classifier.

Example B venues: directly relevant established specialist conferences and journals, to be assessed against the direction's research question. A venue name from Scholar alone is insufficient.
