# Venue and evidence policy

- Primary window: rolling three years from the search date. Verify the actual publication date.
- Core related work: directly relevant formal papers from field-appropriate A/B venues. List this field's A/B venues below and verify the publisher or proceedings record; a Scholar snippet is insufficient.
- Recent exception: work from the last 12 months may enter a **provisional candidate** list from a wider range of conferences, workshops, early access, preprints, or technical reports. Mark status and do not present it as equivalent to strong peer-reviewed evidence.
- Older foundations: mark separately, outside the three-year candidate count.
- Direct novelty threats: retain even from weaker venues, with the reason recorded. Filter after retrieval, not before.
